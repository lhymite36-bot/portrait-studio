# Portrait Studio v1.1.0 (premium tools)

Everything is free, offline and watermark-free. Install **PortraitStudio-1.1.0.apk** below (it updates v1.0.0 in place).

## New
- **Backdrop**: remove the background (transparent PNG), studio gradients, solid colours, or your own photo behind the people.
- **Sky**: replace open sky with Sunset, Blue sky, Clouds, Starry night, Nightglow, Aurora or your own photo (classical sky detection; best on daytime photos with clear sky).
- **Retouch**: natural skin smoothing, eye brighten and teeth whiten (face model downloads once, 3.7 MB), tap-to-heal blemishes.
- **Relight**: a soft movable light: drag it on the photo; size, warmth, intensity, darken background.
- **Erase**: paint over objects or people and remove them (classical fill, best for small/medium objects), with Undo.
- **Restore**: repairs dust and thin scratches, fixes faded contrast, denoises and sharpens; **Colourise** for black and white photos (basic, not AI).
- **Text & stickers**: drag-to-place text (4 fonts, colours, glow, box) and emoji stickers.
- **Effects**: light leaks (Amber, Rose, Cyan, Prism) and film dust, plus the existing glow, vignette, grain and fade.
- **Looks**: import your own **.cube** LUTs, save **My looks**, export your current look as a .cube.
- **Batch edit**: apply the current settings to many photos at once and save them all.
- **Video**: stabilise (normal/strong), cinematic **focus shift** left→right / right→left, Balanced mask mode, and all of the above (backdrop, relight, retouch, LUT, text, leaks, dust) applied to every frame.
- Full-resolution exports with no watermark.
