# Portrait Studio v1.2.0 (Video Studio)

A CapCut-style timeline editor, free, offline and watermark-free. Install **PortraitStudio-1.2.0.apk** below. It updates v1.1.0 in place. Open it from **Open → Video Studio** or the **Studio** button on the start screen.

## New: Video Studio
- **Timeline** with Video, Overlay, Text/Captions and Audio tracks. Tap to select, drag to move, drag edges to trim, plus **Split**, **Duplicate**, **Delete** and **Move ◀ ▶** to reorder. Several clips merge into one video, and photos can be clips too.
- **Transitions**: fade, zoom, whip, glitch and slide, with adjustable length.
- **Keyframes** for overlays and text: position, size, rotation and opacity, with smooth easing.
- **Speed**: 0.25× to 4×, plus speed curves (Montage, Hero, Bullet, Flash in, Flash out, Jump cut).
- **Freeze frame**, **Reverse** and **Duplicate**.
- **Auto captions**: word-by-word timing from Whisper tiny, which runs on the phone. It downloads about 40 MB on first use. Caption styles are Pop, Karaoke, Boxed, Neon and Typewriter, and you can fix the words afterwards.
- **Text and stickers** with pop, fade, slide and typewriter animations.
- **Music and SFX**: 6 royalty-free (CC0) music tracks and 8 sound effects, all generated on the phone. You can also add your own audio. **Extract audio** saves a clip's sound as WAV and moves it to the audio track.
- **Beat sync**: finds the beat of the music and re-cuts your clips on the beat.
- **Voice effects**: chipmunk, deep, robot, echo and telephone. **Reduce noise** is also available.
- **Text-to-speech voice-over** using the phone's own Android voice engine.
- **Chroma key** (green or blue screen) on clips and overlays.
- **Picture-in-picture** overlays with blend modes (screen, multiply, overlay, lighten, difference) and shape masks (rounded, circle, heart, star), plus an optional border.
- **Face zoom** (punch-in on people) and a **glow outline** around people.
- **Auto reframe**: turns 16:9 into 9:16 and keeps people centred. Aspect choices are 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 or the original.
- **Templates**: Beat Montage, Cinematic Trailer, Glow Up, Vlog Day, Zoom Punch and Neon Night.
- **Your Portrait Studio look** (grade, effects, LUT) can be applied to the whole edit.
- **Export**: 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K at 24, 30 or 60 fps, with an optional file-size limit (8, 16 or 50 MB). There is no watermark. 4K and 60 fps only work if the phone's encoder supports them. The preview is low-res so it stays smooth, and exports are rendered at full quality.

## Limits
- Not tested on a real phone yet. The preview plays by seeking and can be choppy on budget phones, but the export is frame-exact.
- The whole video is built in memory. Keep edits to about 3 to 5 minutes at 720p or 1080p on a 4 GB phone.
- Auto captions need internet once to download the model. They are slow on budget phones (about 1 to 2× the clip length).
- Text-to-speech needs a TTS engine installed on the phone (most have Google's). The voice comes from the phone, not AI.
- Noise reduction is a simple noise gate with filters, not AI. Projects are not saved between app restarts.
